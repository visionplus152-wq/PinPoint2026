How the Day Flows

Pickup starts around 7:30–8:00 AM from central Tokyo. First stop: Hakone's torii gate and a pirate-ship crossing of Lake Ashi, then the ropeway over Owakudani's smoking vents. The afternoon turns toward Fuji — swan boats at Lake Yamanaka, then the postcard-famous ponds of Oshino Hakkai — before you're dropped back in Tokyo by early evening.
