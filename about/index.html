About Pinpoint Travel.

Pinpoint Travel is a travel center that puts tours, transportation and places to stay in one simple place, so planning a trip takes less time.

What we do

We gather tour packages, flights, car rentals, transfers and stays from trusted booking partners, and link you straight to them.

How it works

Pick a destination, compare options and book with the partner. You pay the partner directly, never us.

How we earn

Some links are affiliate links. If you book through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Privacy policy.

A short, plain-language summary of how this website handles information.

What we collect

Pinpoint Travel does not ask you to create an account or enter personal details to browse this website. If you email us, we only use your message and email address to reply to you.

Cookies and analytics

This website, and the partner websites you can open from it, may use cookies or similar tools to make pages work, measure visits and track affiliate referrals. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Affiliate links

Some links on this website are affiliate links, including links to partners such as Klook, Tiqets, Go City, GetYourGuide and Travelpayouts. If you book or buy through them, Pinpoint Travel may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Prices and availability are set by the partner.

Third-party websites

When you click a link, you leave our website and go to a partner. Bookings, payments and personal details you enter there are handled by that partner under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Your choices

  • You can use this website without giving us any personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete any message you have sent us.
  • You can control cookies in your browser at any time.

Changes and questions

We may update this page from time to time. If you have a question about it, please contact us.
